AI PROMPT – GREEDY JOB SEQUENCING FLOWCHART

Create a clean and professional academic flowchart for the
Greedy Job Sequencing with Deadlines algorithm.

Use the following example:

J1: Deadline = 2, Profit = 100
J2: Deadline = 1, Profit = 50
J3: Deadline = 2, Profit = 80
J4: Deadline = 1, Profit = 40
J5: Deadline = 3, Profit = 60

The flowchart should show the following steps:

Start
↓
Input jobs with deadlines and profits
↓
Sort jobs in decreasing order of profit
↓
Find maximum deadline
↓
Create empty time slots
↓
Select a job
↓
Check for an available slot before or at its deadline
↓
If slot is available, schedule the job
↓
If no slot is available, skip the job
↓
Check whether all jobs are processed
↓
Calculate total profit
↓
Display selected jobs and maximum profit
↓
End

Use rectangles for processing steps, diamonds for decisions,
and arrows to show the flow. Make the diagram easy to read,
well organized, and suitable for a B.Tech Data Structures and
Algorithms macro project.
