# Greedy Job Sequencing with Deadlines

## Unit II – Divide and Conquer / Greedy

### Objective
To maximize the total profit by scheduling jobs within their deadlines using a greedy approach.

### Problem
Each job has a deadline and a profit. Each job requires one unit of time. The objective is to select and schedule jobs to obtain maximum profit.

### Example

| Job | Deadline | Profit |
|-----|----------|--------|
| J1  | 2        | 100    |
| J2  | 1        | 50     |
| J3  | 2        | 80     |
| J4  | 1        | 40     |
| J5  | 3        | 60     |

### Result
Selected Jobs: J3, J1, J5

Maximum Profit: 240
